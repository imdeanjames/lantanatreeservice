Privacy Policy

This policy describes how information may be used when you browse lantanatreeservice.com or call the tree removal enquiry number shown on the website.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. About this Website

This website provides information about tree removal and facilitates telephone enquiries that may be referred to independent third-party tree removal service providers. It is independently operated and is not the previous business associated with this domain.

2. Information We May Collect

When you call, we may receive the caller telephone number, date, time, call duration and routing information, where made available by our telecommunications provider. If you leave a voicemail, your name, number, location, description of the tree removal job and recorded voice message may be collected. Ordinary hosting systems may collect technical data such as IP address and browser details.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to receive and assess your tree removal enquiry, contact you where appropriate, refer the enquiry to relevant independent providers, track telephone referrals, address misuse and comply with legal obligations.

4. Independent Service Providers and Referrals

Tree removal enquiries and relevant contact details may be shared with independent third-party providers to assist with the services you request. This website may receive compensation for qualifying enquiries or referrals. Providers are responsible for their own work and privacy practices. Calling does not, by itself, constitute consent to unrelated marketing.

5. Call Tracking, Voicemail and Recording

Calls to the number on this website may be processed through third-party telecommunications systems that capture routing and basic call metadata. At present, the intended initial call flow asks callers to press 1 before leaving a voicemail. Voicemail messages are recorded after the recording notice is given. Voicemail content may be reviewed and, where appropriate, shared with independent service providers. The initial voicemail setup does not imply that all live telephone conversations are recorded. If the service later routes calls live or implements further recordings, notices and consent arrangements will be updated as required by applicable law. Do not leave payment information or sensitive personal data in a voicemail.

6. Website Technology and Cookies

This static website does not currently include a contact form or custom advertising trackers. Hosting providers may process normal server logs. If analytics, cookies or advertising tools are subsequently added, this policy and any necessary notices will be updated.

7. Data Sharing and Retention

Information may be processed by telecommunications providers, hosting suppliers and independent service providers involved in an enquiry. Data should be retained only as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, administrative needs, dispute resolution and legal obligations. A specific retention schedule has not yet been established.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. To make a request about information handled by this website operator, contact dean AT djfpublishing DOT com, writing the website name in your message. Separate providers may hold information under their own policies.

9. Security and Third Parties

Reasonable security practices should be used, but no system guarantees absolute security. Independent service providers may have their own terms and privacy practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy as call handling, referral arrangements or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
